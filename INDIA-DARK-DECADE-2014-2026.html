Part 01

Economic Blunders

From demonetization to FDI collapse — the numbers that expose the economic destruction.

1.1 — Demonetization (Nov 8, 2016)

The biggest self-inflicted wound in Indian economic history.

The Stated Purpose vs Reality:

ClaimReality
"Wipe out black money"99.3% of money returned to banks (RBI Annual Report 2018)
"Stop terrorism funding"No evidence of reduction
"Move to digital economy"Cash in circulation increased after

Human Cost: 82+ deaths linked to queues & cash shortages. 1.5 million jobs lost. Employment rate crashed from 42.59% to 41.45%. 40% job losses in age 40-55 segment.

Economic Cost: 1.5-2 percentage point GDP loss. Industrial production declined 20-30%. IMF lowered growth estimate by 1 full percentage point. Sensex fell 6%+ the day after announcement.

1.2 — GST Chaos (July 1, 2017)

  • 230,000+ small businesses shut down due to GST compliance burden
  • Multiple tax slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) — opposite of "One Nation, One Tax"
  • GST Council met 50+ times in first few years to fix broken rates
  • Combined with demonetization, GST "wrecked India's small traders"

1.6 — Farmer Crisis

  • BJP 2014 manifesto promised MSP at C2+50% (50% profit over cost) — government implemented A2+FL (cheaper formula) instead
  • No legal MSP guarantee enacted as of 2026
  • 10,281 farmers/farm labourers died by suicide in 2022 alone — roughly one every hour
  • Over 112,000 farmer suicides in the ~10-year period 2014-2022
  • 726-750 farmers died during the 13-month protest (Nov 2020 – Nov 2021)
  • At least 9 farmer suicides during the farm law protest

1.7 — GDP Manipulation

Arvind Subramanian (former Chief Economic Advisor to Modi government): India's GDP growth overstated by ~2.5 percentage points. Real growth 2012-2017: ~4.5%, not the official ~7%. 2026 update (PIIE): actual growth might be ~3%.

Ashoka Mody (Princeton/World Bank/IMF): India's realistic growth is ~4.5%. The headline 7-8% rests on "statistical sleight-of-hand." Manufacturing data, FDI collapse, and employment figures all contradict the narrative.

1.9 — Make in India Failure

Manufacturing share of GDP: 16.7% (2013-14) → 15.9% (2023-24) — a DECLINE. Target was 25%. PLI scheme launched March 2020 "barely moved the needle." Government quietly let it lapse in early 2025.

Total depreciation: ~63% from ₹59 in 2014 to ~₹97 in 2026. Rupee weakened 11.95% in the last 12 months alone.

1.11 — FDI Collapse

Net FDI fell to just $353 million in FY 2024-25 — one-seventh of the previous lowest in 25 years. Peak net FDI was $44 billion (2020-21). India's share in global FDI: peaked at 6.5% (2020) → crashed to 2.1% (2023). Mauritius (population 1.3 million) remains India's #1 "foreign" investor (25% of inflows) — widely suspected to be round-tripped black money.

2.2 — Opposition Leaders Jailed

  • Arvind Kejriwal (Delhi CM): Arrested March 21, 2024 by ED — first sitting CM arrested by ED. Spent 156 days in jail while serving as CM. Arrested weeks before 2024 elections. Later DISCHARGED of all charges — entire case collapsed.
  • Manish Sisodia (Delhi Deputy CM): Arrested Feb 26, 2023. Spent ~530 days (17 months) in custody. Later DISCHARGED/acquitted — charges collapsed.
  • Hemant Soren (Jharkhand CM): Arrested Jan 31, 2024 by ED. Resigned before arrest. Later re-elected after winning legal battles.

The Pattern: Three opposition Chief Ministers arrested during election season. The cases collapsed. Lives and careers destroyed. Democracy subverted. Join BJP → case dropped ("washing machine").

  • August 2023: Modi government passed law removing CJI — new panel: PM + Cabinet Minister + Leader of Opposition — ruling party gets built-in 2-1 majority
  • March 9, 2024: Election Commissioner Arun Goel resigned weeks before 2024 elections — reduced ECI to one-man commission

2.8 — CBI Director Midnight Coup

Oct 23, 2018 (~1:00 AM): Modi government invoked obscure rule to send CBI Director Alok Verma on forced leave — called a "midnight coup." Jan 8, 2019: SC reinstated Verma — ruled midnight removal illegal. Jan 10, 2019: Just 48 hours later, PM Modi-led committee voted 2-1 to permanently remove Verma. CJI (Ranjan Gogoi) voted with PM. Verma was later listed as a Pegasus surveillance target.

2.9 — NGO Crackdown

  • Greenpeace India, Compassion International, Ford Foundation — all targeted
  • FCRA Amendments 2020: reduced admin expense cap from 50% to 20%, prohibited sub-granting, made Aadhaar mandatory — effectively crippled civil society

Social Catastrophes & Human Cost

The dead, the displaced, the silenced — the human cost of Modi's governance.

4.1 — Manipur Crisis (May 2023-Present)

  • 260+ killed (Crisis Group)
  • 60,000+ displaced into relief camps
  • 4,786 weapons looted from police armories
  • Hundreds of churches and homes destroyed — complete ethnic cleansing
  • Viral video (July 19, 2023): Two Kuki-Zo women paraded naked and sexually assaulted (incident from May 4 — suppressed for 2 months)
  • 20-year-old gang rape survivor died January 2026 without seeing justice. Amnesty: "devastating indictment of the Indian state's continuing failure"
  • Modi did not address crisis for ~80 days — never visited Manipur throughout

4.2 — Cow Vigilante Lynchings

May 2015 - Dec 2018: At least 44 killed, 36 of them Muslims (82%). ~280 injured in 100+ incidents across 20 states. 90% of attacks occurred after BJP came to power. 66% in BJP-ruled states. Muslims were victims in 62% of cases.

4.3 — Delhi 2020 Riots

  • 53 killed (36 Muslims, 15 Hindus)
  • 200+ injured, 2,200+ arrested
  • Triggered by BJP leader Kapil Mishra's incendiary "ultimatum" speech
  • NYT: "If We Kill You, Nothing Will Happen" — police complicity documented
  • Investigation disproportionately targeted Muslim activists and student leaders under UAPA

4.4 — Kashmir: 552 Days of Darkness

August 4, 2019: Communications blackout. No internet for 213 days (until partial 2G March 4, 2020). 4G not restored until February 5, 2021 — approximately 552 days total. Declared the longest internet shutdown in any democratic country. Kashmir Chamber of Commerce: $2.4 billion losses, 500,000 job losses.

  • All major political leaders arrested: Farooq Abdullah (under PSA), Mehbooba Mufti, Omar Abdullah
  • 300+ arrested under Public Safety Act (detention up to 2 years without trial)
