# PART 5: MODI FOREIGN VISITS + AMBANI/ADANI CONTRACTS

## Overview

As of July 2026, PM Modi has made **103 international trips to 82 countries**. In the first four years (2014-2018): 41 trips to 52 countries over 165 cumulative days, costing the exchequer **Rs 355 crore** (RTI disclosures).

Companies controlled by Gautam Adani and Anil Ambani signed **18 agreements in 16 countries** during Modi's first term alone — **13 deals by Adani Group** and **5 deals by Anil Ambani's Reliance Group (ADAG)**.

> **Note:** The correlation between Modi's visits and Adani/Ambani deals is widely reported by investigative journalists. The Indian government and both conglomerates deny any impropriety, stating these are legitimate business deals.

## A. DEFENCE DEALS

### 1. FRANCE — Rafale Fighter Jets (Ambani)
- **Modi Visit:** April 9-12, 2015 (France)
- **Deal:** 36 Rafale fighter jets in flyaway condition
- **Amount:** **Rs 58,000 crore (~$7.87 billion)**
- **Beneficiary:** Anil Ambani's Reliance Defence (incorporated March 28, 2015 — 12 days before Modi's announcement). Named as offset partner for Dassault.
- **Status:** Supreme Court cleared it December 2018. Offset partnership later terminated by Dassault (2019).

### 2. SWEDEN — Fighter Jets & Naval UAVs (Adani & Ambani)
- **Modi Visit:** April 16-18, 2018 (Sweden)
- **Deals:**
  - March 2015 (Ambani): Reliance Defence + **Saab AB** for Indian Navy UAV programme — **Rs 1,000 crore**
  - September 2017 (Adani): Saab tie-up with Adani Group for 100+ single-engine fighter aircraft — estimated **Rs 60,000+ crore**

### 3. ISRAEL — Unmanned Aircraft & Missiles (Adani & Ambani)
- **Modi Visit:** July 4-6, 2017 (first-ever India PM visit to Israel)
- **Deals:**
  - March 2016 (Adani): Adani Aero Defence + **Elbit-ISTAR** for unmanned aircraft systems
  - March 2016 (Ambani): Reliance Defence + **Rafael Advanced Defence Systems** for air-to-air missiles — **~Rs 65,000 crore** (JV: 51% Reliance / 49% Rafael)

### 4. RUSSIA — Air Defence Systems (Ambani)
- **Modi Visits:** July 2015; December 2015; May-June 2017
- **Deal:** Reliance Defence + **Almaz-Antey** (S-400 makers) — finalized at **$5.4 billion (Rs 39,000 crore)** during Putin's visit October 2018

### 5. USA — Warship Repair (Ambani)
- **Modi Visit:** June 6-8, 2016 (USA)
- **Deal:** February 2017 — Reliance Defence + **US Navy** for Seventh Fleet repair at Pipavav shipyard, Gujarat
- **Expected Revenue:** **Rs 15,000 crore** over 3-5 years

## B. PORT & LOGISTICS DEALS (Predominantly Adani)

### 6. AUSTRALIA — Carmichael Coal Mine (Adani)
- **Modi Visit:** November 14-18, 2014 (G20 Summit, Brisbane)
- **Deal:** Queensland infrastructure for Galilee Basin coal; MoU with SBI for **Rs 6,200 crore loan**
- **Status:** SBI loan never materialized. Mine operating at **1/6th** capacity (10M tonnes/year vs 60M proposed)

### 7. SRI LANKA — Container Terminal & Wind Power (Adani)
- **Deals:**
  - **West Container Terminal:** $700 million terminal at Colombo Port (construction began November 2022)
  - **Wind Power:** $442 million wind power projects in Mannar and Pooneryn (signed March 2022)
- **Status:** Both criticized for lack of competitive bidding. Sri Lanka amended electricity law to eliminate bidding for projects >10 MW. New President Dissanayake vowed to cancel wind project.

### 8. BANGLADESH — Godda Power Plant (Adani)
- **Deal:** Adani Power's 1,600 MW plant dedicated to exporting power to Bangladesh. Bangladesh buys **1,496 MW for 25 years**
- **Key Terms:** Bangladesh pays **5x** the country's average electricity cost. Even if plant produces no electricity, Bangladesh liable for **$450 million/year** capacity charges for 25 years
- **Status:** Transparency International Bangladesh called contract "unequal, opaque and discriminatory." Post-Hasina government reviewing deal.

### 9. ISRAEL — Haifa Port (Adani)
- **Modi Visit:** July 2017 (Gautam Adani was in Modi's entourage)
- **Deal:** January 2023 — Adani-led consortium (70% Adani, 30% Gadot) acquired Haifa Port for **$1.15-1.8 billion**

### 10. MALAYSIA — Carey Island Port (Adani)
- **Modi Visit:** November 21-22, 2015
- **Deal:** Adani + MMC Ports for **Carey Island Port Project** — integrated maritime city, expenditure **$32 billion**

### 11. KENYA — Airport & Power Lines (Adani)
- **Context:** Kenyan President Ruto met Modi December 5, 2023. Gautam Adani was surprise attendee.
- **Deals:**
  - **Airport:** $1.85 billion for 30-year lease of Jomo Kenyatta International Airport
  - **Power Lines:** $736 million-$1.3 billion for high-voltage transmission lines
- **Status:** **ALL CANCELLED** by Kenya November 2024 after US indictment of Adani. Kenyan MPs applauded cancellation.

### 12. IRAN — Chabahar Port
- **Modi Visit:** May 22-23, 2016
- **Deal:** India committed **$500 million** for Chabahar port development
- **Status:** Adani Ports shortlisted but India operates via India Ports Global

### 13. MYANMAR — Yangon Port (Adani)
- **Deal:** Adani Ports signed ~**$290 million** deal for Yangon container port
- **Status:** Criticized for links to Myanmar military. Adani **exited Myanmar** 2022, sold for just **$30 million** (massive loss)

### 14. JAPAN — Auto Freight Transport (Adani)
- **Modi Visit:** Adani accompanied Modi on visits
- **Deal:** July 2018 — Adani Logistics + **NYK Auto Logistics** (Japan) JV for vehicle transport

### 15. INDONESIA — Coal Imports (Adani)
- **Deal:** Adani partnered with Indonesian mining firms
- **Status:** Subject of DRI investigation into alleged "over-invoicing" — inflated coal import prices 3x+ through tax haven routing, raising electricity bills

# SUMMARY: KEY WINS AND AREAS OF CONCERN

## Major Gains (2014 to 2024/25)
- GDP nearly doubled ($2.04T to $3.9T)
- Forex reserves more than doubled ($304B to $701B)
- UPI revolution: 0 to 18 billion monthly transactions
- FDI: $81B annually vs $24-36B (143% decade increase)
- Defense exports: 34x increase
- Renewable energy: 35 GW to 258 GW (7x growth)
- Infrastructure: highways tripled, airports doubled, rail electrified
- 171 million lifted from extreme poverty (World Bank)
- Sensex tripled (25,000 to 75,000+)

## Areas of Stagnation or Decline
- Manufacturing share: 16.7% to 15.9% (25% target missed)
- Press freedom: 140th to 159th
- Corruption perception: ranking worsened 85th to 96th
- Global Hunger Index: 102-105th, "serious"
- Coal dependency persists at ~73% of electricity
- Healthcare spending still low at ~1.6-2.2% of GDP
- Farmer suicides continue (~10,500/year)
- Rupee depreciated ~39% (Rs 61 to Rs 85 per USD)

## KEY CAVEATS

1. **Alleged vs Proven:** Correlation between Modi's foreign visits and Adani/Ambani deals is **alleged by critics** but **not legally proven** as corruption. Government and both conglomerates deny any impropriety.

2. **ED Statistics:** 93.33% conviction rate reflects completed trials only. Political cases have ~1% conviction rate. Both figures are technically accurate but measure different things.

3. **ECI:** SIR upheld by Supreme Court. Allegations of voter manipulation remain unproven in court but have significantly affected public trust.

4. **Hindenburg Report (Jan 2023):** US short-seller alleged Adani Group was "the largest con in corporate history" — $150B+ market value loss. Adani denied all allegations. US DOJ indictment (Nov 2024) charged Gautam Adani with securities fraud — ongoing legal matter.

5. **Source Bias:** Some sources (NewsClick, AdaniWatch, National Herald) have editorial positions critical of the government. Government sources (ED website, PIB) present favorable framing. This document presents both perspectives with attribution.
