How It Started

From "Cockroaches" to a Movement

A judge's insult. A leaked exam. 11 student suicides. This is how a meme became a revolution.

May 15, 2026

CJI Calls Youth "Cockroaches"

Chief Justice of India Surya Kant compared unemployed youth to "cockroaches" and "parasites" in open court. Triggered nationwide outrage.

May 16, 2026

CJP Born

Abhijeet Dipke (30, Boston University graduate) founded the Cockroach Janta Party — a satirical movement flipping the insult into pride.

May 12, 2026

NEET Paper Leak

2.27 million aspirants took NEET-UG. Exam cancelled after 120-140 questions matched a "guess paper" sold for Rs 5-10 lakh per student.

Post-Leak

11 Student Suicides

11+ NEET aspirants died by suicide between cancellation and re-test. One father: "The system killed my son."

Al Jazeera, Hindustan Times

The Day

July 20: Hour by Hour

From dawn barricades to midnight re-occupation.

4:00 AM

Dawn Crackdown

Delhi Police raids Jantar Mantar. Protesters removed. Stage dismantled. Section 163 BNSS imposed.

6:00 AM

5 Metro Stations Shut

Rajiv Chowk, Patel Chowk, Central Secretariat, Udyog Bhawan, Janpath sealed by DMRC.

9:00 AM

Thousands Defy Orders

Despite prohibitory orders, 50,000+ protesters gather. Crowd swells rapidly.

11:25 AM

Tear Gas & Lathi Charge

Police open tear gas. Lathicharge begins. Electrified barricades. Lathis with nails used on students.

12:00 PM

Mass Detentions

~70 protesters detained. Dragged into buses. Families can't contact them. DCP slaps woman protester on camera.

3:00 PM

Opposition Detained

Rahul & Priyanka Gandhi detained near Modi's residence. Rahul physically dragged. Nosebleed visible.

10:00 PM

Re-Occupation

Protesters return to cleared site. Dipke reclaims the stage. Movement escalating.
