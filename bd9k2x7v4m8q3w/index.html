How It Started

From "Cockroaches" to a Movement

A judge's insult. A leaked exam. 11 student suicides. This is how a meme became a revolution.

May 15, 2026

CJI Calls Youth "Cockroaches"

Chief Justice of India Surya Kant compared unemployed youth to "cockroaches" and "parasites" in open court. Triggered nationwide outrage.

May 16, 2026

CJP Born

Abhijeet Dipke (30, Boston University graduate) founded the Cockroach Janta Party — a satirical movement flipping the insult into a badge of pride.

May 12, 2026

NEET Paper Leak

2.27 million aspirants took NEET-UG. Exam cancelled after 120-140 questions matched a "guess paper" sold for Rs 5-10 lakh per student.

Post-Leak

11 Student Suicides

11+ NEET aspirants died by suicide between cancellation and re-test. One father: "The system killed my son."

Al Jazeera, Hindustan Times
The Day

July 20: Hour by Hour

From dawn barricades to midnight re-occupation. Every key moment documented.

4:00 AM

Dawn Crackdown Begins

Delhi Police raids Jantar Mantar. Protesters removed. Stage dismantled. Section 163 BNSS imposed across central Delhi.

6:00 AM

5 Metro Stations Shut

Rajiv Chowk, Patel Chowk, Central Secretariat, Udyog Bhawan, Janpath sealed by DMRC. Major roads barricaded.

9:00 AM

Thousands Defy Orders

Despite prohibitory orders, 50,000+ protesters gather near Jantar Mantar. Crowd swells rapidly.

11:25 AM

Tear Gas & Lathi Charge

Police open tear gas shells. Lathicharge begins. Electrified barricades deployed. Protesters pelted with stones from trucks.

12:00 PM

Mass Detentions Begin

~70 protesters detained. Dragged into buses. Families unable to contact them for hours. DCP Sandeep Lamba caught on video slapping a woman protester.

3:00 PM

Opposition Leaders Detained

Rahul & Priyanka Gandhi stage sit-in near Modi's residence. Both physically detained. Rahul dragged away — nosebleed visible.

5:00 PM

Nadda Meeting Fails

CJP meets BJP's JP Nadda for 4 hours. Zero demands met. Ranka: "Wasted 4 hours for this useless meeting."

10:00 PM

Re-Occupation of Jantar Mantar

Protesters return to the cleared site. Dipke reclaims the stage. Movement not dying — escalating.
