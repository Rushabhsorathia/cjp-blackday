## 1.1 DEMONETIZATION (November 8, 2016) — The Biggest Self-Inflicted Wound

PM Modi announced demonetization of all ₹500 and ₹1,000 notes at 8 PM on Nov 8, 2016 — wiping out **86% of currency** overnight.

### The Stated Purpose vs Reality

| Claim | Reality |
|-------|--------|
| "Wipe out black money" | **99.3% of money returned** to banks (RBI Annual Report 2018) — ₹15.3 lakh crore out of ₹15.41 lakh crore |
| "Stop terrorism funding" | No evidence of reduction |
| "Move to digital economy" | Cash in circulation *increased* in following years |

### Human Cost
- **82+ deaths** linked to queues, stress, and cash shortages
- **1.5 million jobs lost** — employment fell from 406.7M to 405M (CMIE)
- Employment rate crashed from **42.59% to 41.45%**
- **40% job losses** in age 40-55 segment (All India Manufacturers' Organisation)
- **32% job losses** in age 22-30 segment

### Economic Cost
- **1.5-2 percentage point GDP loss**
- Industrial production declined **20-30%** in immediate aftermath
- RBI surplus declined **53.46%**
- Sensex and Nifty fell **6%+** the day after
- IMF lowered India's growth estimate by **1 full percentage point**

*Sources: RBI Annual Report 2018, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, CMIE, IMF, Quartz India*

## 2.3 ELECTORAL BONDS SCAM — Legalized Bribery

### The Scheme
- Introduced via **Finance Act 2017** as a money bill (to bypass Rajya Sabha)
- Allowed **anonymous, unlimited corporate donations** to political parties
- Removed requirement that donors be profit-making entities
- Amended FCRA to allow **foreign companies** to donate

### The Numbers
- **₹12,769 crore** (~$1.5 billion) in bonds purchased 2018-2024

- **~1,300 entities** purchased bonds across 23-24 parties

### Quid Pro Quo Evidence
- Many donors were companies under **active ED/CBI/IT investigation**

- Distorted level playing field
- **Despite this, Modi defended the scheme** in interviews

### Post-Bonds (2024-25)
